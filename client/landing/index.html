How one video gets made

Every step is visible in the dashboard. You can stop at any step, change a scene, and continue.

  1. Finds a topic

    From your own topic list, or from what is trending on Google and YouTube in your country — matched to your channel's niche, never repeating recent videos.

  2. Writes the script

    A hook in the first seconds, short scenes, your tone and language. Hindi, English or Hinglish. Checked for quality before going further.

  3. Draws each scene

    One image per scene, planned from what the narration says. Images with unwanted writing are caught by a text check and redrawn.

  4. Records the voice

    Natural Hindi or English voices. One voice for the whole video — if a voice service fails, the whole video is re-recorded with your backup voice.

  5. Renders the Short

    Vertical 9:16 video with subtitles, timed to the narration, followed by automatic checks on length, audio and file.

  6. Uploads with SEO

    Title, description, tags and hashtags written for search, then uploaded to your channel — public, private or scheduled at your best times.

Everything it does

Create

  • Channel profile. Niche, audience, tone, hook style, length, language, visual style and topics to avoid — every video follows it.
  • Trending topic ideas. Suggestions from Google Trends and YouTube trending, filtered for your niche.
  • Scenes that match the words. Each image is planned from its narration line — one moment, one idea, people and places that fit your audience.
  • Hindi voices. Microsoft, Google, ElevenLabs, AI Awaaz, Gemini and more, with a voice of your choice.
  • Subtitles and vertical video. Ready for YouTube Shorts.

Publish

  • Autopilot. Creates and uploads up to twice a day at times you choose, fully automatic or waiting for your approval.
  • SEO for every upload. Search-friendly title, description, tags and hashtags, reviewed before upload.
  • Upload settings per channel. Privacy, category, made-for-kids, AI-content label, playlist, schedule and description footer.
  • Approval step. Watch the video, check the scenes, approve — or let Autopilot do it.

Control from Telegram

  • Updates on your phone. Video ready, uploaded, failed — with the video attached.
  • Approve with one tap. Right from the “ready” message.
  • Change a scene. Describe a new image or narration line, see the new image, then render.
  • Commands. Create a video, list recent or pending videos, upload, schedule, delete.

Reliable and private

  • Backup providers. Put AI services in order; if one is busy or out of quota, the next one takes over.
  • Free AI services supported. Gemini, Groq, Mistral, OpenRouter, Cloudflare, GitHub Models and others.
  • Your keys stay encrypted. API keys and YouTube access are stored encrypted and never shown again.
  • Live progress. See each step, how long it is taking, and exactly what went wrong if something fails.

Questions

Do I pay anything every month?

No. The plan is a one-time payment. The AI services have free tiers that are enough for a few videos a day; if you choose paid services, they bill you directly.

Which languages does it support?

Scripts and voices in Hindi, English and Hinglish. Other languages work for scripts; voice support depends on the voice service you pick.

Will my videos be uploaded without me checking them?

Only if you turn on fully automatic Autopilot. Otherwise every video waits for your approval in the dashboard or in Telegram.

Is it allowed by YouTube?

It uploads through YouTube's official API with your permission, and can mark videos as AI-generated content. You are responsible for following YouTube's policies and for the content on your channel.

Can I add more channels later?

Yes. Upgrade to a bigger plan at any time and pay only the difference.

What happens to my API keys?
