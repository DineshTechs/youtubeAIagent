Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what information AI Video Manager ("we", "us") handles when you visit aividman.store, buy a plan, or use the AI Video Manager software ("the app"), and what choices you have. If you have a question, reach us through the contact details on our home page.

Summary

  • The app makes and uploads videos to your YouTube channel, only after you connect it and only for that purpose.
  • Your YouTube access and API keys are stored encrypted and are never sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI.
  • You can disconnect YouTube and delete your data at any time.

1. Information we handle

Website visitors

The public pages of this website (home, privacy and terms) use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site: pages viewed, device and browser, approximate location (city level), how you arrived, and actions such as clicking a plan. These tools use cookies or similar identifiers; Google processes this data under the Google partner-sites policy. You can block these cookies in your browser or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The dashboard (the app itself) does not use them, and no YouTube or Google account data is sent to analytics. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested, time) for security and troubleshooting.

Customers

When you contact us or buy a plan we receive what you send us: your name, email address or phone/WhatsApp number, the plan you chose and payment confirmation. Payments are handled by the payment service you use; we do not see or store full card or bank details.

Inside the app

  • Account: the admin password, stored only as a secure one-way hash, and a sign-in cookie.
  • YouTube / Google account data: when you connect a channel, Google gives the app an access token and a refresh token, and the app reads your channel's name and ID. These tokens let the app upload videos you approve (or that your Autopilot settings allow), set their title, description, tags, thumbnail and schedule, and read basic channel and video details shown in the dashboard.
  • API keys you add for AI services (for example Gemini, Groq, Cloudflare, OpenAI, AI Awaaz, ElevenLabs). They are stored encrypted and shown only as their last four characters.
  • Content you create: channel profiles, topics, scripts, narration, images, audio, videos, upload settings, logs and quality reports.
  • Telegram (optional): if you connect a Telegram bot, its token and the chat IDs you allow.

2. How we use information

  • To make videos: write scripts, create images and narration, render and check videos.
  • To upload and schedule videos on the YouTube channel you connected, and show their status to you.
  • To send you notifications you turned on (for example in Telegram).
  • To provide support, deliver your purchase, keep the service secure and fix problems.
  • To measure visits to the public website and improve it (website analytics only).

We do not sell personal information, do not use it for advertising, and do not use your YouTube data or content to train AI models.

3. YouTube API Services and Google user data

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload and manage videos on your channel. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

AI Video Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features you see in the app, is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features, for security, or to comply with law, and is never used for advertising.

You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account security settings or with Disconnect in the app. When access is removed, the stored tokens are deleted or stop working, and channel data the app saved is deleted within 30 days unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

4. Services that process data for us

To make a video, the app sends the needed text, prompts or audio to the AI services you choose and add keys for (for example script, image, voice and speech-to-text services). Those services process the data under their own terms and privacy policies, with your account. Other providers we rely on: Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics (website visits only), our web host, our database host (MongoDB Atlas), Google/YouTube for uploads, Telegram if you turn it on, and Cloudflare if you use it.

5. Where data is stored and how it is protected

Data is stored on our web host and database host. Access to the app requires a password; connections use HTTPS; API keys and YouTube tokens are encrypted at rest; sign-in is rate-limited. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you without undue delay if a breach affects you.

6. How long we keep data

  • Videos, images and audio: until you delete them, or as set in the app's storage clean-up settings.
  • YouTube tokens and API keys: until you disconnect or remove them.
  • Customer and purchase records: as long as needed for your licence, support and legal or tax requirements.
  • Server logs: kept for a short period for security.

7. Your choices and rights

You can see, change, export or delete your data in the app, disconnect YouTube, and remove API keys at any time. You can also ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or withdraw consent, by contacting us through the contact details on our home page. Where the law gives you more rights (for example India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR), we will honour them.

8. Children

The service is not meant for children under 13, and plans may only be bought by adults (18 or over). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for important changes, tell customers by email, WhatsApp or in the app.

10. Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact AI Video Manager through the contact details on our home page. We aim to reply within 7 days and resolve complaints within 30 days.
